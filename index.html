遇到问题时 · Getting help

01

连接与离线

远程操作需要目标电脑、网络及 Agent 会话保持运行。电脑关机、手动睡眠或网络断开时，Acoda 不能替代开机或远程唤醒。

The connected computer, network and agent must remain available. Acoda does not power on or wake a computer remotely.

02

账号与授权

请确认正在使用预期的 Acoda 账号。模型、GitHub、Lark 或云端工作区可能需要各自的有效账号和授权；不要在聊天或支持邮件中发送密码、Token 或私钥。

Check the account you signed in with. Optional services require their own valid access. Never send passwords, access tokens or private keys to support.

03

会话与任务状态

如果历史未加载或任务状态与电脑不同，请记录发生时间、App 版本和显示的错误。结果不确定时，先核对目标 Agent，避免重复执行同一任务。

Include the time, app version and visible error when reporting a history or task-state issue. Verify uncertain operations before repeating them.

CONTACT

我们需要哪些信息？

设备型号、iOS / iPadOS 版本、Acoda 版本、重现步骤及已隐去敏感信息的截图即可。请不要发送完整私人会话、验证码或登录凭据。

Send your device and OS version, Acoda version, steps to reproduce and a redacted screenshot. Do not send full private conversations or credentials.
