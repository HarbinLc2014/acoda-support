遇到问题时 · Getting help

01

连接与离线

本地远程操作需要目标电脑、网络及 Agent 会话保持运行。电脑关机、手动睡眠或网络断开时，Acoda 不能替代开机或远程唤醒。独立云端工作台需要相应云服务在线，休眠机器可能先显示唤醒页面。

Local remote control needs the computer, network and agent to remain available. Acoda does not power on or wake a local computer. An independent cloud workspace needs its cloud service online and may show a wake-up page for a sleeping machine.

02

账号、授权与删除

请确认正在使用预期的 Acoda 账号。模型、GitHub、Lark 或云端工作区可能需要各自的有效账号和授权；不要在聊天或支持邮件中发送密码、Token 或私钥。

提供 App 内删除功能的版本可在“我”→“删除 Acoda 账号”中确认删除。旧版本没有入口时请先更新，仍不可用可通过下方邮箱联系支持。此操作不注销独立服务账号，也不删除电脑原文件或独立 DSH 的原生会话；详见删除范围。

Check your Acoda account. Optional services require their own access; never send credentials to support. In versions with in-app deletion, use Me → Delete Acoda Account. Update older versions without this entry or contact support if unavailable. This does not close independent service accounts or remove computer files or independent DSH history. See the deletion scope.

03

会话与任务状态

如果历史未加载或任务状态与电脑不同，请记录发生时间、App 版本和显示的错误。结果不确定时，先核对目标 Agent，避免重复执行同一任务。

Include the time, app version and visible error when reporting a history or task-state issue. Verify uncertain operations before repeating them.

CONTACT

我们需要哪些信息？

设备型号、iOS / iPadOS 版本、Acoda 版本、重现步骤及已隐去敏感信息的截图即可。请不要发送完整私人会话、验证码或登录凭据。

Send your device and OS version, Acoda version, steps to reproduce and a redacted screenshot. Do not send full private conversations or credentials.
