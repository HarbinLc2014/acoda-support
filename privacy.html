Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日；更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日
Effective: October 5, 2026. Updated: October 7, 2026.

Acoda 由 Chang Lu 运营，用于连接和管理你授权的开发 Agent 会话。本政策说明 Acoda 在账号、会话同步、远程操作及相关支持中的数据处理。它不替代你连接的模型、代码托管或协作服务的政策。

Acoda is operated by Chang Lu and connects and manages development-agent sessions you authorize. This policy describes data handling for accounts, session synchronization, remote operations and support. It does not replace the policies of model, code-hosting or collaboration services you connect.

1. 处理哪些信息 · Information processed

  • 账号信息：注册邮箱、用户标识、你提供的昵称和头像，以及登录所需的认证信息。使用 Apple 登录时，会处理 Apple 提供的账号标识及你选择分享的姓名或邮箱。
  • 连接信息：已关联电脑或云端工作区的标识、名称、平台、连接状态，以及授权绑定和连接凭据或其验证信息。
  • 会话内容：接入或导入的历史记录、会话标题、工作目录、模型信息、指令和消息、Agent 输出、任务状态、时间戳及同步事件。这些内容可能包含代码或其他个人信息。
  • 附件与反馈：你发送的图片、文件及相关元数据，以及主动提供给支持人员的错误信息、设备和版本信息、截图或问题说明。

Processed information includes account email and identifiers, profile details you provide and authentication information; connected device or workspace identifiers, names, platform, status, authorization records and connection credentials or verification material; connected or imported session history, titles, working directories, model details, instructions, messages, agent output, task status, timestamps and synchronization events; and images, files, associated metadata and support information you submit. Apple sign-in provides an identifier and the name or email you choose to share. Session content can contain code or personal information.

2. 用途 · Purposes

这些信息用于注册与登录、关联你授权的设备和服务、发送指令、同步任务进展和历史、处理附件、恢复连接，以及处理支持请求和保护账号访问。远程指令可能在你授权的电脑或云端工作区内读取或修改文件；请在发送前确认目标会话及其权限。

Information is used for registration and sign-in, associating authorized devices and services, delivering instructions, synchronizing task progress and history, handling attachments, reconnecting, responding to support requests and protecting account access. Remote instructions may read or modify files in an authorized computer or cloud workspace. Check the target session and its permissions before sending instructions.

3. 云端与第三方 · Cloud and third parties

Acoda 使用 Supabase 提供账号认证、数据库和云存储。账号、设备关联、同步的会话内容和附件可能存储或处理在云端，而不只保留在手机上。当前 Acoda 云端项目位于新加坡；第三方服务的其他处理地点取决于相应提供方。

当你启用模型、云端工作区、GitHub 或 Lark 等集成时，完成该操作所需的指令、上下文、附件或授权信息会交由相应服务处理。将报告发送到 Lark 后，所选接收者也会收到报告及其附件。请只接入你有权使用的资料，并查看相应服务的隐私政策和账号设置。

Acoda uses Supabase for authentication, database and cloud storage. Account records, device associations, synchronized session content and attachments may be processed or stored in the cloud, not solely on your phone. The current Acoda cloud project is in Singapore; other processing locations depend on the providers involved. When you enable model, cloud-workspace, GitHub or Lark integrations, information needed for that operation is processed by the relevant service. A Lark report and its attachments are also shared with its selected recipients. Connect only information you are authorized to use and review each provider's policies and account settings.

提供“云端工作台”网页入口的版本会直接打开你选择并有权访问的独立 DSH 服务，其原生会话、项目文件和模型授权由该服务管理；这个入口不经由 Lark 消息转发，也不把网页会话内容另行同步到 Acoda 的会话数据库。其他已启用的同步或集成仍可能按照上文处理数据。

App 内云端网页的登录数据（例如 Cookie 和缓存）按 Acoda 账号分别保存在设备上；保存的工作台地址也按账号区分。Acoda 不从网页提取或注入模型 Token。系统浏览器、Lark App 与云端服务有各自的登录状态，不会因切换 Acoda 账号而自动切换。附件通过网页原生文件选择器上传；下载会先写入 App 的临时目录，只有你点击“存储或分享”并选择目标后才交付给所选位置或应用。

Versions with the cloud-workspace web entry open the independent DSH service you select and are authorized to access. That service manages its native sessions, project files and model authorizations. This entry does not relay messages through Lark or separately synchronize web-session content into Acoda's session database; other enabled synchronization or integrations may still process data as described above. Embedded website sign-in data, including cookies and caches, and saved workspace addresses are separated by Acoda account on the device. Acoda does not extract or inject model tokens from that page. The system browser, Lark app and cloud service maintain their own sign-in state, which does not automatically change when you switch Acoda accounts. Uploads use the website's native file picker. Downloads use an app temporary directory and are exported to a destination or app only after you choose Store or Share and select that destination.

4. 保留与删除 · Retention and deletion

账号和同步历史用于持续提供服务；退出 App、退出登录或断开设备不等于删除云端记录。在提供 App 内删除功能的版本中，可登录后打开“我”→“删除 Acoda 账号”，阅读删除范围并确认。旧版本如果没有这个入口，请先更新；若仍无法使用，或需要访问、更正及部分数据删除，请通过下方隐私联系方式说明请求范围。我们不会要求你通过邮件发送密码或 Token。

App 内账号删除针对 Acoda 控制的该账号资料、设备关联、同步会话记录、指令与事件、相关附件及远控授权。处理未完成、网络中断或本机缓存清理失败时，界面会说明状态；不要把退出登录或超时当成删除成功。请求可能需要重试以继续清理，少量删除回执用于识别同一次请求和恢复处理，不用于重建已删除会话。

删除确认后，App 会清理这个 Acoda 账号在本机保存的工作台地址和隔离网页登录数据；关闭工作台会取消在途下载并清理其临时文件。你已导出到“文件”、相册、其他应用或其他设备的副本不由这一步删除。若本机清理失败，请按 App 提示关闭工作台并重试，或通过系统删除 App 的本机数据。

删除 Acoda 账号不会注销独立的 DSH、GitHub、Lark、Apple 或模型提供方账号，也不会删除电脑上的代码、Agent 原生历史、独立 DSH 服务保存的会话与项目文件，或第三方平台已经收到的消息和附件。请在相应服务中管理这些数据、撤销授权或提出删除请求。手机列表中删除一条会话也不代表上述原始资料已被删除。

收到其他隐私请求后，我们会验证账号归属，处理 Acoda 控制范围内的相关记录，并说明可能需要保留的信息，例如安全调查、备份恢复或适用法律要求涉及的记录。备份中的副本可能在备份轮换期间继续保留；本页不对未核实的备份或第三方保留期限作承诺。

Closing the app, signing out or disconnecting a device does not delete cloud records. In versions offering in-app deletion, sign in and open Me → Delete Acoda Account, review the scope and confirm. If an older version lacks this entry, update first. If it remains unavailable, or for access, correction or partial-data requests, contact us below and describe the scope; never email passwords or tokens. In-app deletion covers that account's Acoda-controlled profile, device associations, synchronized history, commands and events, associated attachments and remote-control grants. The interface reports incomplete processing, connection errors or local cleanup failures; signing out or a timeout is not proof of successful deletion. A retry may be needed to continue cleanup. Limited deletion-receipt information identifies a retry of the same request and does not reconstruct deleted conversations.

After deletion is confirmed, the app clears that Acoda account's saved workspace address and isolated website sign-in data on the device. Closing a workspace cancels active downloads and clears their temporary files. Copies already exported to Files, Photos, other apps or other devices are outside this cleanup. Follow any local-cleanup error shown by the app or remove the app's local data through the operating system. Deleting Acoda does not close independent DSH, GitHub, Lark, Apple or model-provider accounts, or remove computer files, native agent history, sessions and projects stored by an independent DSH service, or messages and attachments already delivered to third parties. Manage or request deletion of those records and revoke authorizations through the corresponding service. Removing one conversation from the phone does not delete all such source records. Other privacy requests require ownership verification; we will explain records that may need to remain for security investigations, backup recovery or applicable legal obligations. Backup copies may persist during backup rotation. This page does not promise an unverified backup or third-party retention period.

5. 你的选择 · Your choices

你可以停止发送内容、不启用可选集成，或通过相应服务的账号设置撤销授权。撤销授权不会自动收回已发送的消息或文件。你也可以联系我们申请访问、更正或删除个人信息、咨询数据处理或撤回相关同意；我们会根据适用要求处理，并说明对账号或功能的影响。验证身份不需要你发送密码、验证码、Token 或私钥。

You can stop sending content, leave optional integrations disabled or revoke access through the relevant provider's account settings. Revocation does not recall messages or files already sent. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, ask about processing or withdraw relevant consent. We will handle requests according to applicable requirements and explain any effect on your account or features. Identity verification does not require sending us passwords, verification codes, tokens or private keys.

6. 安全与使用边界 · Security and boundaries

Acoda 的云端访问使用账号认证和数据访问控制。远程开发会话可能具有较高权限；不要在不可信设备上授权，不要在提示词、附件或支持邮件里放入不必要的密钥。本服务不作“零数据收集”或“所有内容端到端加密”的承诺。任何网络服务都不能保证绝对安全。

Cloud access uses account authentication and data-access controls. Remote development sessions may have significant privileges. Do not authorize untrusted devices or include unnecessary secrets in prompts, attachments or support emails. This service does not promise zero data collection or end-to-end encryption of all content. No network service can guarantee absolute security.
